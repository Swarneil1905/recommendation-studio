Online Retail historical sample
Source: Chen, D. (2015). Online Retail. UCI Machine Learning Repository.
https://doi.org/10.24432/C5BW33
https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/352/online-retail
License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Adaptation: positive-quantity, positive-price merchandise lines from November
2011. Cancellations and non-merchandise codes excluded. Orders projected onto
the 100 most frequently ordered products that month. Keep projected baskets
with 1 to 30 products; take the first 2,000 eligible invoices in ID order.
Order IDs replaced with sequential sample IDs; descriptions title-cased.
Customer IDs, countries, dates, quantities and prices are not included.
This selection changes the distribution and is not representative of all
customers or current demand. It must not be used to claim expected sales uplift.
Rebuild with tools/prepare_retail_sample.py and the original UCI ZIP.

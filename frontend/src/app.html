A CLOSER LOOK

Know what you're testing.

Can I buy these products?

No. The sample catalog contains fictional products with generated product imagery. This is a tool for exploring search and recommendations, not a shop or checkout.

What does the similarity score mean?

It measures overlap between product descriptions, your query, and selected preferences. It is not a confidence percentage, a quality rating, or a prediction that someone will buy.

How do I use my own catalog?

Choose “Bring your own products” and download the JSON example. Each item needs an id, name, category, price, stock, and description. Upload up to 500 items in a file under 1 MB. Imported products use category illustrations when no sample image applies.

Will my data be saved?

Uploaded catalogs are processed for your requests and remain in the current browser tab. They do not replace the public catalog and are cleared when you reload or leave. Export results before closing the workspace.

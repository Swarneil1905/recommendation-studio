What data do I need?

A CSV with exactly order_id, product_id and product_name columns. Include 10 to 5,000 orders and up to 200 distinct products. Repeated quantities in an order count once.

Is this machine learning?

The bundle finder uses pairwise association rule mining. It learns purchase co-occurrence from your data and reports support, confidence and lift. It also evaluates hidden-item retrieval on a separate order subset.

Are my orders saved?

No. The app processes the submitted order IDs and product IDs for the request. Names remain in your browser. Reloading clears the workspace; export your shortlist to keep it.

Will it update my shop automatically?

No. The output is a bundle shortlist for review and testing. Current inventory, prices and profit margins must be checked in your own store.
